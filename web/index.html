How it works

Each new module is written from scratch (never copied from Clavia) and added to the OS with its registration, its parameters and lights, and its DSP56300 code. The OS then runs it as it runs Clavia's own.

  1. You give the builder your own 3.03 ROM. We never distribute Clavia's firmware.
  2. It adds the modules you tick, on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. The result says version 3.09, so Animatek NME (0.25 or later) knows that synth has the new modules and offers them only to it.
  4. A G1 with Clavia's OS keeps working exactly as before.

Build your OS

New module types for the Nord Modular G1, built into your own copy of its OS, here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: your ROM and the result never leave this computer.

Experimental on a real G1. On one real Nord Modular G1, all the modules have played in one build. A modified OS may lose your patches and may leave your synth unusable. Recovery is not guaranteed. You use it entirely at your own risk.

Tick it to unlock the steps below.

1Your ROM

The rack's 3.03 ROM, the one G1-Emu boots (NORD-MODULAR-RACK-VER-3.03.BIN, 512 KB). We do not give it: it is Clavia's.

OS update: for G1-Emu and for a real G1
The OS alone, as an update image. It loads only through update mode, the same on both: NME's Device > Update Synth OS, with the G1 in update mode (G1-Emu: Settings > OS... > Update mode; a real G1: Shift + Store held at power-on).
⬇ OS update
ROM: for G1-Emu only
The same OS inside a whole 512 KB ROM. It loads in G1-Emu alone, from Settings > ROM > Choose... (it restarts with it); a real G1 cannot take it, and NME's Update Synth OS refuses it.

Help build it

G1-Mods is a passion project and it grows from what people bring to it. Pull requests are welcome and you do not need to ask first. Take a module from the checklist, write it, test it in G1-Emu first, and your name goes on it: in the module's author field, in the changelog line, and in the notes of the release that includes it.

How to contribute The checklist (PORTING.md) Issues

The setup, how a module is laid out, the limits of the G1, your first pull request and the house rules are in CONTRIBUTING.md, in the repository, so they travel with the code. The short version: nothing of Clavia's in Git, everything in English, test in the emulator before any real synth, and the way back always exists.
