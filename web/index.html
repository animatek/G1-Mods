New module types for the Nord Modular G1, built into your own copy of its OS, here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: your ROM and the result never leave this computer.

Experimental on a real G1. One build has run on one real Nord Modular G1: the FlipFlop alone. An earlier build hung at power-on until Clavia's OS was sent again. Only a build of four or five modules fits an OS update; every module is tested in the G1-Emu emulator. A modified OS may lose your patches and may leave your synth unusable. Recovery is not guaranteed. You use it entirely at your own risk. An independent, unofficial project, not affiliated with Clavia.

4Install

In G1-Emu (the safe way):

  1. Close G1-Emu.
  2. Point it at the ROM you built (Settings, or G1-Emu rom.bin flash.bin) with a flash of its own, so your usual patches are not touched. If its settings name an OS file (os =), leave that empty: that OS would boot instead of the one in the ROM.
  3. Start it and connect Animatek NME: it sees version 3.09 and offers the new modules.

In a real G1 (experimental, at your own risk; back up your patch banks first; only when the page gives you an OS update file):

  1. Hold Shift + Store while switching the G1 on: it waits in MIDI update mode.
  2. In Animatek NME: Device › Update Synth OS, pick the OS update file, speed Safe (about 12 minutes). Do not touch the cable or the synth meanwhile.
  3. When the G1 asks “Update flash?”, press Store (any other key keeps the old OS) and wait for Update completed.
  4. Switch it off and on. While trying the new modules, send patches to a slot; do not store them in a bank.

The way back: Shift + Store at power-on and Clavia's 3.03b update (its own updater, or NME with its file). Shift + Patch/Load at power-on boots the ROM's factory OS, never the one in the flash.
